- Examination fee: Rs 1,500 per semester.
- Backlog (re-appear) paper: Rs 600 per paper.

4. Eligibility
- At least 75 percent attendance in each subject, or approved condonation.
- No pending university dues.

5. Corrections
- Mistakes in the exam form can be corrected until 23 November 2026 at the Examination
  Cell for Rs 200.

6. Admit card
- Admit cards are available on the student portal from 1 December 2026.
- Students must carry the admit card and the college ID card to every exam.

7. Revaluation
- Apply within 7 days of the result declaration.
- Fee: Rs 800 per paper.
