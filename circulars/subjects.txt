GLA UNIVERSITY, MATHURA - ACADEMIC AFFAIRS
Circular No: GLA/ACAD/2026/024
Date: 29 October 2026
Subject: B.Tech subjects and subject codes
(Sample circular created for the hackathon demo. Not real data.)

1. B.Tech subjects and their codes (the five subjects listed for this demo)
1. Design and Prototyping - subject code BDPG 0001
2. Mathematics - subject code BAMS 0002
3. Science for Engineering - subject code BCHS 0002
4. Industrial Automation - subject code BIAG 0001
5. Cyber Security - subject code BSCS 0607

2. Where subject codes are used
- Exam form: select each subject by its code, for example "Cyber Security (BSCS 0607)".
- Attendance records and the attendance shortage list.
- Admit card and result.
- Backlog (re-appear) and revaluation applications.
- Condonation applications.

3. Rules that apply to every subject
- At least 75 percent attendance is required in each subject (circular GLA/ACAD/2026/021).
- Condonation for 65 to 74 percent attendance costs Rs 500 per subject.
- A backlog (re-appear) paper costs Rs 600 per paper.
- Revaluation costs Rs 800 per paper and must be applied for within 7 days of the result.
- Always quote the subject name together with its code in emails and applications.
  Example: "Industrial Automation (BIAG 0001)".

4. Other subjects
- This circular lists only the five subjects above. For any other subject, ask the
  student's Head of Department or Academic Affairs.
- Syllabus, credits, faculty and timetable are not part of this circular.
