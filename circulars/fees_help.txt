Date: 29 October 2026
Subject: Fee help - checking dues, pending payments, double payments and fee certificate errors
(Sample circular created for the hackathon demo. Not real data.)

1. How to check your pending college fees
Step 1: Log in to the student portal with your roll number and password.
Step 2: Open the "Accounts" or "Fees" section and choose "Fee Statement" (also called
        "Pending Dues").
Step 3: Read the semester-wise table. It shows the total fee, the amount paid, the balance
        (pending amount), the due date and any late fee added so far.
Step 4: Check the hostel and mess fees separately, because they are listed on their own lines.
Step 5: Click "Download Statement" to save a PDF copy for your records.
- The balance shown is the amount you must pay. If the due date has passed, the late fee of
  Rs 100 per day (maximum Rs 2,000) is already included.
- If you cannot see the Fees section, or the amount looks wrong, email accounts@gla.example.edu
  with your name, roll number and a screenshot of the page, or visit the Accounts Office
  (Admin Block) with your ID card between 10:00 and 16:00 on working days.
- Having no pending dues is required for filling the exam form and for getting the admit card.

2. Payment is successful but the website still shows it as pending
1. Do NOT pay again. Wait 24 hours, because bank confirmation can take that long. It may take
   up to 2 working days on weekends and holidays.
2. Keep proof: the transaction ID (UTR or reference number), the date and time, the amount,
   the bank SMS or debit entry in your bank statement, and a screenshot of the success page.
3. Check whether the amount was really debited from your account. If it was not debited,
   the payment failed and you must pay again.
4. If the amount was debited and the portal still shows "Pending" after 2 working days, email
   accounts@gla.example.edu with: full name, roll number, transaction ID, date and time,
   amount, and the screenshot or bank statement entry.
5. The Accounts Office checks with the payment gateway and updates your account within
   3 working days. You will get a reply by email.
6. If the exam form or admit card date is close, tell the Accounts Office and the Examination
   Cell at once and carry your bank proof. A payment made before the due date is not treated
   as late once the proof is verified.
7. If it is still not updated after 7 working days, contact the Student Services Office.

3. Fees paid twice by mistake
- Contact the Accounts Office (accounts@gla.example.edu or the counter in the Admin Block)
  within 30 days of the payment.
- Bring or send: your roll number, both transaction IDs, the dates and amounts, and the bank
  statement showing both debits.
- After verification, the extra amount is refunded to the same bank account or card that was
  used for payment, within 7 to 10 working days. No charge is deducted for a duplicate payment.
- If you prefer, you can ask in writing to have the extra amount adjusted against your next
  semester fee.

4. Fee payment certificate shows an incorrect amount
1. Do not submit or use the certificate.
2. Email accounts@gla.example.edu or visit the Accounts Office with: the incorrect certificate,
   your fee receipts for that semester, your ID card and your roll number.
3. The Accounts Office compares the certificate with the fee ledger.
4. A corrected certificate is issued within 2 working days. There is no charge if the mistake
   was made by the university. If the mistake came from wrong details given by the student,
   the normal certificate fee applies.
