GLA UNIVERSITY, MATHURA - ACCOUNTS OFFICE
Circular No: GLA/ACC/2026/014
Date: 28 October 2026
Subject: Fee schedule for academic year 2026-27
(Sample circular created for the hackathon demo. Not real data.)

1. Tuition fee per semester
- B.Tech (all branches): Rs 60,000
- BCA / BBA: Rs 35,000
- MBA: Rs 75,000

2. Other fees per semester
- Library and laboratory fee: Rs 4,000
- Examination fee: Rs 1,500 (paid when the exam form is filled)
- Hostel, double occupancy: Rs 45,000
- Mess charges: Rs 24,000

3. Due dates
- Odd semester (July to December): 20 November 2026
- Even semester (January to June): 31 January 2027

4. Late fee
- Rs 100 per day after the due date, up to a maximum of Rs 2,000.
- If fees remain unpaid 15 days after the due date, the student's name may be removed
  from the rolls unless the Dean approves an extension.

5. How to pay
- Online through the student portal (UPI, net banking, debit or credit card).
- Demand draft in favour of "GLA University".
- Cash is not accepted at the accounts counter.

6. Receipts
- A receipt is generated automatically on the student portal after online payment.
- A duplicate receipt can be collected from the Accounts Office for Rs 50.

7. Refund
- 90 percent of the tuition fee is refunded if the student withdraws before classes start.
- 50 percent is refunded if the student withdraws within 15 days of classes starting.
- No refund after 15 days.

8. Instalments
- Students with annual family income below Rs 3 lakh may pay in two instalments.
- Apply to the Accounts Office with an income certificate by 10 November 2026.

For fee queries: accounts@gla.example.edu
