GLA UNIVERSITY, MATHURA - SCHOLARSHIP CELL
Circular No: GLA/SCH/2026/005
Date: 26 October 2026
Subject: Scholarships for academic year 2026-27
(Sample circular created for the hackathon demo. Not real data.)

1. Merit scholarship
- First-year students: the top 5 percent in the entrance test receive a 50 percent
  tuition fee waiver.
- Continuing students: CGPA of 9.0 or above receives a 25 percent tuition waiver.
- Continuing students: CGPA from 8.5 to 8.99 receives a 10 percent tuition waiver.

2. Need-based scholarship
- Students whose annual family income is below Rs 2.5 lakh receive a 30 percent
  tuition fee waiver.
- An income certificate is required.

3. Sports scholarship
- State-level players: 20 percent tuition waiver.
- National-level players: 40 percent tuition waiver.

4. Government scholarships (SC, ST, OBC and minority schemes)
- Apply on the National Scholarship Portal.
- The Scholarship Cell verifies the application. Submit printed copies of the
  application for verification.

5. How to apply for university scholarships
- Last date: 5 December 2026.
- Documents: income certificate, last marksheet, bank passbook copy, Aadhaar copy,
  latest fee receipt.
- Submit at the Scholarship Cell, Admin Block, Room 112.

6. Conditions to continue
- At least 75 percent attendance and no backlog papers.
- A scholarship may be withdrawn if these conditions are not met.

For scholarship queries: scholarship@gla.example.edu
