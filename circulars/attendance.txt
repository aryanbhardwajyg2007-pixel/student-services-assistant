GLA UNIVERSITY, MATHURA - ACADEMIC AFFAIRS
Circular No: GLA/ACAD/2026/021
Date: 20 October 2026
Subject: Attendance rules for academic year 2026-27
(Sample circular created for the hackathon demo. Not real data.)

1. Minimum attendance
- A student must have at least 75 percent attendance in each subject to be allowed
  to sit the end-term examination.

2. Condonation
- Students with 65 to 74 percent attendance in a subject may apply for condonation.
- A valid reason is required, for example a medical certificate.
- The Head of Department must approve the application.
- Condonation fee: Rs 500 per subject.

3. Detention
- Students below 65 percent in a subject are detained in that subject and cannot sit
  the exam. Only the Dean may allow an exception in exceptional cases.

4. Medical leave
- Submit the medical certificate to the class coordinator within 7 days of returning
  to class.

5. Duty leave
- Attendance at university events, sports or competitions counts as present if the
  Head of Department approves it before the event.
- Duty leave is counted for a maximum of 10 percent of classes in a semester.

6. Warnings and records
- Attendance is published weekly on the student portal.
- Students whose attendance falls below 80 percent receive a warning email.
- Mistakes in attendance records must be reported to the class coordinator within
  3 working days of publication.
