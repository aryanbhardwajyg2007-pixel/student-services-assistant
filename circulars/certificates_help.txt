GLA UNIVERSITY, MATHURA - REGISTRAR'S OFFICE
Circular No: GLA/REG/2026/014
Date: 29 October 2026
Subject: Certificates - documents required, corrections and applying for more than one
(Sample circular created for the hackathon demo. Not real data.)

1. Documents required for a bonafide certificate
- College ID card (if it is lost, the fee receipt of the current semester).
- Roll number and the purpose of the certificate (for example bank loan, passport, visa or
  scholarship).
- If the certificate is for a bank or another organisation, the name of that organisation.
- Collect it at the Registrar's Office, Counter 2, with your ID card.

2. Documents required for a fee payment certificate
- College ID card and roll number.
- Fee receipts of the semester or year for which the certificate is needed.

3. Documents required for a character certificate
- College ID card and roll number.
- A no-dues slip from the Accounts Office.
- Confirmation that no disciplinary action is pending against you.
- The signature of the Head of Department (the Registrar's Office gets it after your request).
- One recent passport-size photograph.

4. Documents required for a migration certificate
- Only for students who have completed their final year.
- Final-year result or marksheet, college ID card and roll number.
- No-dues slips from the Accounts Office, the Library and the hostel (if you stayed there).
- Two recent passport-size photographs.
- Fee: Rs 300.

5. Spelling mistake in a migration certificate (or any certificate)
1. Do not use the certificate.
2. Go to the Registrar's Office, Counter 2, within 30 days of issue. Bring: the certificate,
   proof of the correct spelling (for example the 10th class marksheet or Aadhaar card),
   your ID card, and a short written request.
3. If the mistake was made by the university, the corrected certificate is free. If it came
   from wrong details given by the student, a reissue fee of Rs 300 applies.
4. The corrected migration certificate is issued within 7 working days.

6. Applying for a character certificate and a migration certificate together
- Yes, you can apply for both at the same time.
- Submit a separate request for each certificate. Each request gets its own request ID.
- You can use the same documents for both. The processing times run side by side, so both
  are usually ready within 7 working days (character: 5 working days, migration: 7).
- The migration certificate fee of Rs 300 applies. Remember that a migration certificate
  is issued only after the final year is completed.

7. Fee payment certificate showing a wrong amount
- See the fee help circular GLA/ACC/2026/017, section 4.
