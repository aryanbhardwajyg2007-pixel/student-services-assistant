Subject: Scholarship help - status, rejection, payment delays and corrections
(Sample circular created for the hackathon demo. Not real data.)

1. How to check the status of your scholarship application
1. Log in to the student portal and open the "Scholarship" section, then "Application Status".
2. The status is one of: Submitted, Under Verification, Verified, Approved, Sent for Payment,
   Paid or Rejected.
3. For government scholarships, also check the status on the National Scholarship Portal
   using your application ID.
4. Verification normally takes up to 15 working days after you submit.
5. If the status has not changed for more than 15 working days, email
   scholarship@gla.example.edu with your name, roll number and application ID, or visit the
   Scholarship Cell (Admin Block, Room 112).

2. If your scholarship application is rejected
1. Open the application and read the rejection reason. Common reasons are a missing or
   unclear document, an expired or invalid income certificate, attendance below 75 percent,
   a backlog paper, or bank details that do not match.
2. If the reason can be fixed, collect the correct documents and submit a written appeal
   with them at the Scholarship Cell (Room 112) within 7 days of the rejection.
3. The Scholarship Cell reviews the appeal and tells you the result within 10 working days.
4. If you are not eligible (for example the CGPA or income limit is not met), you can apply
   again in the next scholarship cycle.

3. Scholarship amount not credited to your bank account
1. First check that the status shows "Sent for Payment" or "Paid". Approval alone does not
   mean the money has been sent.
2. After "Sent for Payment", the amount normally reaches the bank account within 7 working
   days. Government scholarships depend on when the funding agency releases the money.
3. Common reasons for a delay: wrong or inactive account number or IFSC code, an account
   that is not enabled for direct benefit transfer, a name that does not match the bank
   record, or the funding agency has not released the amount yet.
4. Check with your bank that the account is active and enabled for direct benefit transfer.
5. If the status is "Paid" but the amount is missing after 7 working days, email
   scholarship@gla.example.edu with your roll number, application ID, the payment date shown on
   the portal and only the last 4 digits of your account number. Do not email the full
   account number.

4. How to correct an error in your scholarship application
- Before the last date (5 December 2026) and while the status is "Submitted": open the
  application on the portal and use the edit option, then submit again.
- After verification has started: submit a written correction request with a supporting
  document at the Scholarship Cell (Room 112). Name or date-of-birth corrections need proof
  such as a school marksheet.
- Corrections are not accepted after the scholarship is approved, except for bank details
  (see below).

5. Wrong bank account number in your application
1. Fill the Bank Detail Correction Form at the Scholarship Cell (Room 112).
2. Attach: a copy of the first page of your passbook or a cancelled cheque showing your name,
   account number and IFSC code, a copy of your ID card and your application ID.
3. The account must be in the student's own name.
4. The correction is processed within 5 working days.
5. If the money has already been sent to a wrong account, tell the Scholarship Cell and the
   Accounts Office immediately so that the bank can be asked to recall the payment. The
   recall cannot be guaranteed.
