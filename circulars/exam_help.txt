GLA UNIVERSITY, MATHURA - EXAMINATION CELL
Circular No: GLA/EXAM/2026/012
Date: 29 October 2026
Subject: Exam help - fee deducted but form not submitted, confirmation receipt, admit card problems
(Sample circular created for the hackathon demo. Not real data.)

1. Examination form fee deducted but the form is not submitted
1. Do NOT pay again. A gateway delay can leave the form incomplete even though the money was
   debited.
2. Wait 24 hours and check the "Exam Form" status on the student portal again.
3. Confirm the debit in your bank statement or SMS and keep: the transaction ID, date and
   time, amount, and a screenshot of the error or success page.
4. If the form is still not submitted, email examcell@gla.example.edu with your name, roll
   number, semester, transaction ID, date and time, amount and the screenshot.
5. The Examination Cell verifies the payment and completes the form within 2 working days.
6. A payment made before the last date is not counted as late once the proof is verified.
   If the last date (25 November 2026) is close, visit the Examination Cell in the Admin
   Block with your ID card and the bank proof on the same day.

2. How to download the examination form confirmation receipt
1. Log in to the student portal and open "Examination", then "Exam Form".
2. Check that the status shows "Submitted". The receipt is available only after submission.
3. Click "Download Confirmation Receipt" to save the PDF, and keep a printed copy.
4. If the download option is missing, the form is still incomplete or the payment is not
   confirmed yet. Follow the steps in section 1 or email examcell@gla.example.edu with your
   roll number.

3. Exam fees paid but the admit card cannot be downloaded
Admit cards are available from 1 December 2026. If you cannot download yours, check these in order:
1. Exam form status: it must show "Submitted". If not, follow section 1.
2. Pending dues: any pending university fee blocks the admit card. Check your fee statement
   on the portal and clear the dues (see the fee help circular GLA/ACC/2026/017).
3. Attendance: below 75 percent in any subject, without approved condonation, blocks the
   admit card.
4. Missing photo or signature: upload it in your portal profile if the portal asks for it.
5. Payment still pending: if your exam fee shows as pending, see the fee help circular.
6. If none of these applies, email examcell@gla.example.edu with your roll number and a
   screenshot of the error. If your exam is within 48 hours, go to the Examination Cell in
   person with your ID card and the exam fee receipt.

For exam queries: examcell@gla.example.edu
