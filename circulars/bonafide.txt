GLA UNIVERSITY, MATHURA - REGISTRAR'S OFFICE
Circular No: GLA/REG/2026/011
Date: 22 October 2026
Subject: Bonafide and other certificates
(Sample circular created for the hackathon demo. Not real data.)

1. Bonafide certificate
- Proof that the student is currently enrolled at the university.
- Used for bank loans, passport, scholarships, visa and similar needs.
- Issued within 3 working days.
- The first copy in each semester is free. Each additional copy costs Rs 50.
- Urgent bonafide certificate: same day, with an urgent fee of Rs 100.

2. Fee payment certificate
- Shows the fees paid by the student.
- Issued within 2 working days.

3. Character certificate
- Needs the signature of the Head of Department.
- Issued within 5 working days.

4. Migration certificate
- Only for students who have completed their final year.
- Issued within 7 working days.
- Fee: Rs 300.

5. How to apply
- Type "I want a bonafide certificate" in this assistant (or use the Request a
  certificate button) and answer the questions: full name, roll number and
  certificate type. The same steps work for the fee payment, character and migration
  certificates: choose the certificate type when the assistant asks.
- Keep your ID card and roll number ready when you collect the certificate.

6. Collection
- Registrar's Office, Counter 2, between 10:00 and 16:00 on working days.
