BACKGROUND PICTURE (optional)

To show a GLA University, Mathura campus picture behind the app:
1. Save a picture here and name it exactly:  background.jpg
   (background.png or background.webp also work)
2. Keep the file under 3 MB. Wide (landscape) pictures look best.
3. Refresh the browser tab.

Use your own photo, or one you are allowed to use. If this folder has no
background picture, the app shows a navy gradient instead.
